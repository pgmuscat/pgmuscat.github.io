Hazy memories. A $100,000 fee. No sign of an exit.

Golfing got you into this mess, but maybe golfing can get you out of it.

From the very normal mind of Luke Muscat (Fruit Ninja, Jetpack Joyride), Normal Golf Game is a sadistically accurate recreation of the noble game of golf. Use physics to swing your club, while simultaneously doing like 3 other things.
